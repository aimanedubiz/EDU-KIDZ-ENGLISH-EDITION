Game Guide

1. Money Topics: Seven topics cover the four operations, combined operations and problem solving.

2. Question Formats: Choose from multiple choice, matching, drag and drop, or fill in the blank.

3. Difficulty Levels:
🌱 Easy: Calculate with small amounts in whole ringgit.
⚡ Medium: Calculate with ringgit and sen.
🔥 Hard: Work with amounts up to RM100,000 and solve multi-step problems.

4. Scoring: Each correct answer earns 5 marks. Incorrect answers do not deduct marks.
