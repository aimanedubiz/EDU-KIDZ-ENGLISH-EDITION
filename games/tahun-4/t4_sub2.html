About Math Kids Bernama Edu Kidz

Practise Year 4 mathematics from the Malaysian Ministry of Education’s revised KSSR curriculum. Each round draws questions from the selected topic and difficulty level.

10 Topics:

  • 1. Converting Improper Fractions and Mixed Numbers
  • 2. Adding Fractions
  • 3. Subtracting Fractions
  • 4. Adding and Subtracting Fractions
  • 5. Fractions of a Quantity
  • 6. Adding and Subtracting Decimals
  • 7. Multiplying and Dividing Decimals
  • 8. Converting Fractions and Percentages
  • 9. Percentages of Objects
  • 10. Everyday Problem Solving

Scoring:

Each correct answer earns 5 marks. There are 20 questions per round.
