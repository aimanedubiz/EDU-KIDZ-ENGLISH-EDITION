Game Guide

Year 5 KSSR Mathematics: Time

1. Subtopics

There are five subtopics. Each has 20 questions at each of three difficulty levels. The questions cover minutes, hours, days, months, years and decades.

2. Questions and Scoring

Each question is worth 5 marks. Mixed sessions contain 20 questions. Single-format sessions contain 5 questions.

3. Game Formats

Choose Multiple Choice, Matching, Drag & Drop or Fill in the Blank. A worked explanation appears after each question.
